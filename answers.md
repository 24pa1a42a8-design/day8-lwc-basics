# Revision Answers

## 1. What is a component?
A reusable independent UI building block.

## 2. Why are reusable components useful?
They reduce duplicate code and improve maintainability.

## 3. Difference between frontend and backend?
Frontend handles UI, backend handles business logic and data processing.

## 4. Why did Salesforce move toward LWC?
For better performance and modern web standards support.

## 5. Why is UI important in enterprise systems?
It improves usability and user productivity.

## 6. Why separate UI and business logic?
Improves maintainability and scalability.

## 7. What security risks exist in enterprise applications?
Unauthorized access, data leaks, and insecure validations.

## 8. Why should developers think modularly?
Modular systems are easier to maintain, scale, and debug.
